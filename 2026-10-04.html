TACO: a ternary, one-sparse optimizer for LLM fine-tuning

TACO proposes a ternary, column-wise one-sparse optimizer aimed at reducing optimizer-state memory during full-parameter LLM fine-tuning. The authors target a practical tension: Muon can cut memory but changes the update geometry relative to AdamW, while conventional first-order methods retain expensive state.
