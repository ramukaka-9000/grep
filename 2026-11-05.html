SLAC scientists find conclusive proof of a third type of magnetism

After ~90 years of the ferromagnet/antiferromagnet dichotomy, SLAC researchers report the first conclusive experimental signature of altermagnetism — a third magnetic order — using specialized X-ray measurements. Altermagnets promise the stability of antiferromagnets with spin-polarized currents, a combination spintronics has wanted for decades. This is the milestone paper the field was waiting for.
