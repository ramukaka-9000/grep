OpenAI’s Navier–Stokes blowup, with Lean

OpenAI says an internal model, described as more capable than GPT-6 Astra, produced an analytical proof that smooth 3D incompressible Navier–Stokes flow can form a finite-time singularity under a smooth external force with finite energy, Clay statements C and D. Ax Bui, Huang, Ying arXiv › Recommended

Kalman Delta Networks for linear-attention memory

Ngoc Bui, Tinglin Huang, and Rex Ying recast recurrent associative memory as a linear-Gaussian state-space model so a Kalman gain can weight each delta-rule write by accumulated uncertainty. Exact covariance tracking is a dense Riccati recursion, so they give scan-friendly Diagonal and Isotropic KDN approximations whose uncertainty maps are Möbius and therefore associative. Delta-style updates appear as the special case that drops covariance tracking. Controlled pretraining at 750M and 1.3B parameters is reported to beat listed linear-attention baselines on perplexity and mean downstream accuracy.

Ax Voronkova, Trofimov, Dmitriev, Tulchinskii, Burnaev, Barannikov arXiv › Recommended

CodeTD: attention topology vs code hallucinations

Daria Voronkova and colleagues score code-LLM generations before execution by running topological data analysis on attention maps, treating prompt–generation mismatch as a topological pattern. They evaluate HumanEval, MBPP, BigCodeBench, and MultiPL-E across five languages and ten models up to 34B parameters. The paper reports that CodeTD beats recent baselines and transfers between coding benchmarks. It is a pre-execution filter, not a proof that the generated program is correct.

openai/NavierStokesAndEuler holds Lean 4.34.0-rc2 formalizations matching OpenAI’s Navier–Stokes and Euler write-ups. copperhead: an agent that edits real KiCad files

copperhead is an Apache-2.0 CLI that takes a `brief.md` and runs eight gated stages from spec through KiCad schematic, layout, gerbers, firmware, and a bring-up plan, stopping if ERC/DRC or an on-disk artifact gate fails. Hosted Cloud/Team tiers start at $49 per user per month; the CLI stays free.

Tiny TNOs that still remember how they formed

Hubble (visible) and Webb (infrared) jointly characterized 27 newly found trans-Neptunian objects, some of the faintest directly seen, in two Astronomical Journal papers. Colors of the smallest bodies match their larger siblings in both dynamically cold and hot populations, so collisions have not obviously reset surfaces. Size distributions of the two populations are similar; there are fewer very small bodies than some formation models expected. The smallest measured object is about 5 km across, roughly five times below the best ground-based limit.

The UN encourages Equal Earth maps

Wikipedia records that the UN General Assembly voted in September 2026 to encourage equal-area world maps and specifically noted the 2018 Equal Earth projection of Bojan Šavrič, Bernhard Jenny, and Tom Patterson. Al Jazeera and AFP date the vote 4 September 2026; the US and EU posted explanations of vote. Equal Earth keeps relative area, unlike Mercator, while remaining less polar-stretched than Gall–Peters. The Crash at Crush, 15 September 1896

On 15 September 1896 the Katy railroad staged a head-on crash of two uncrewed locomotives at a pop-up town called Crush, 14 miles north of Waco, to use obsolete 30-ton engines. About 40,000 people came on $3.50 excursion fares; no gate fee was charged. Both boilers exploded and flying debris killed two spectators and injured at least six. Scott Joplin’s Great Crush Collision March followed the same year.

NA Mike DiCicco / NASA NASA › Recommended

A NASA stretch algorithm that found Angkor Wat murals

Decorrelation stretch, documented at JPL in 1996 by Ronald Alley for ASTER on Terra, exaggerates color contrast in digital images. Rock-art hobbyist Jon Harman, a medical-imaging engineer, read that paper around 2005 and shipped a DStretch plugin for NIH ImageJ, plus later phone apps. An archaeologist used it between 2010 and 2012 to recover about 200 faded paintings at Angkor Wat, including riders and musicians in the central tower. Harman says he still fills about 200 DStretch requests a year.

The American “cheetah” was a fish-eating puma cousin

Wikipedia already treats Miracinonyx as a Pliocene–Pleistocene North American felid closer to the cougar than to true cheetahs, with cheetah-like limbs as convergence. ScienceDaily, covering a 4 September 2026 Current Biology paper (doi:10.1016/j.cub.2026.07.072) led by Molly Cassatt-Johnstone and Beth Shapiro, reports nuclear paleogenomes and isotopes from 23–31 ka Yukon fossils. Northern animals look like fish specialists, while Wyoming and Florida animals look like grassland generalists, extending the range about 20 degrees of latitude. Those dietary claims are the paper’s, not the older genus page.
