VP ≠ VNP over every field, formalized in Lean by an Anthropic research model

An internal Anthropic research Claude produced a lower-bound proof that permanent and Hamiltonian-cycle circuits need super-polynomial-subexponential size over every field, separating VP from VNP without settling P vs NP. How should an LLM see a computer? Screenshots, accessibility trees, and ID clicks measured

Across 172 OSWorld tasks and seven OpenAI models, accessibility trees and click-by-ID roughly tripled scores for weaker models (GPT-5.2: 15.8% to 52.8%) while barely moving the strongest ones. Nearly 50 years after launch, Voyager 1 has become the first spacecraft whose signals take a full 24 hours to reach Earth. The probe keeps returning interstellar data on dwindling power, a living record of how far a 1977 design can go.

Iron Age ruler Verica, whose exile triggered Rome's invasion of Britain, may have been a woman

Bournemouth archaeologist Miles Russell argues that 'rex' on Verica's coins was a non-gender-specific Roman title and that the coins depict a feminine horse-riding figure, making the exiled Atrebates ruler a woman whose appeal to Claudius set the AD 43 invasion in motion. The case rests on reinterpretation of coinage and names rather than a new find, so it will be debated.

Sci University of Arkansas Science › Recommended

Moon dust 3D-printed into LEGO-like bricks without any binder

Using low-power laser powder bed fusion, researchers fused simulated lunar regolith into interlocking cross- and H-shaped blocks with no binding agent, publishing in npj Advanced Manufacturing. The bricks are still weaker than binder-based alternatives and untested in vacuum and low gravity, but binder-free in-situ construction removes a major supply problem for lunar bases.

A 50-year-old theory that evolution shields essential genes from mutation fails a clean test

Analyzing the largest mutation-accumulation datasets to date in E. coli and baker's yeast, Bath researchers found no evidence that essential genes mutate less often, in PLOS Biology. Earlier support appears to have been an artifact of 'ghost mutations' removed by selection before measurement.

Interstellar solar sails hit drag from their own drive beam at 75% of light speed

A Harbin Institute of Technology analysis on arXiv finds that beyond roughly 0.75c, diffusely scattered photons from a sail's own propulsion beam transfer momentum backwards, producing drag. The effect adds a previously unappreciated constraint to laser-sail starship designs.
