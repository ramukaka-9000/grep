Astra can read and write weights to improve Qwen

An early set of experiments tests whether a language model can inspect and edit neural-network weights directly, rather than relying only on prompts or retraining. The authors report targeted changes in small networks and a limited improvement to one math model, but are careful to show cases where edits did not help. Their experiment logs are public, making this an unusually inspectable claim about a new model-editing interface.

How we halved DNS build delay and cut memory use by 85%

Cloudflare replaced many whole-zone rebuilds with a planner that maps each DNS change to the smallest build it can prove is safe. It reports 40% fewer full builds, 85% lower peak memory, and half the build delay. The interesting systems detail is how it handles replica lag and Kafka ordering without risking dropped customer changes.

XLA from the Trenches: Correctness on TPU Workloads at Scale

Google researchers describe a large-scale process for finding and triaging correctness bugs in the XLA TPU compiler. It combines generated programs with production traces to expose subtle numerical errors and memory corruption that ordinary compiler checks may miss. This is a useful look at verification infrastructure behind expensive training and serving workloads.

Ax Jie Ren and Zheng Wang arXiv › Recommended

AgentPRO: Coordinating Compiler Optimizations for LLM Agent Workflows

AgentPRO treats an agent workflow as something a compiler can optimize, separating decisions that require model judgment from operations that can be executed directly. The motivation is to avoid repeatedly sending execution details through the model when those values do not affect the next decision. The paper proposes a hierarchical representation for planned duties, generation regions, and concrete operations.

Why we're moving our warehouse off DuckDB

PostHog explains why building a customer-facing data warehouse around DuckDB became difficult as it tried to speak the broad and quirky Postgres protocol expected by client libraries. Maintaining compatibility for many clients consumed effort that the team wanted to spend on the warehouse itself. It is a candid case study in the hidden cost of compatibility, not a claim that DuckDB is generally unsuitable.

This measurement-driven investigation asks whether ordinary Zener diodes can protect circuits from fast electrostatic-discharge surges. NASA’s Hubble Uncovers Case of Missing Oxygen in Nearby Dwarf Galaxies

A Hubble study of 37 nearby dwarf galaxies found substantially less oxygen than researchers expected. The result raises questions about how these small galaxies retain or lose the elements produced by massive stars. NASA’s report links to the peer-reviewed paper published in The Astrophysical Journal on November 2, 2026.

NASA’s CHAPEA Crew “Returns” to Earth

Four crew members emerged on October 31, 2026, after spending more than a year in NASA’s simulated Mars mission. They tested resource limits, communication delays, crop care, and other demands of a long-duration stay without leaving Earth. NASA says the experience will inform planning for future Moon and Mars missions.
