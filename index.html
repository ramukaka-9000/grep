Tokenisation is not in APX

The authors prove that, unless P=NP, no polynomial-time algorithm can approximate the optimum tokenizer objective within any constant factor for several common tokenization formulations. The result reframes tokenizer design as a hard optimization problem, with practical implications for how much to expect from automated vocabulary search.

Can image generation help visual reasoning? An empirical study

Across 13 vision-language model and image-generator pairs, adding a generated helper image reduced average accuracy by 3.7 percentage points and hurt nine of ten tested tasks. The paper finds exceptions after repeated edits, but its main result cautions against assuming that generated visual aids improve multimodal reasoning.

TinyBrainBot-360M-A75M-v6: a from-scratch 383M-parameter MoE language model

This base model has 383M total parameters and activates about 75M per token across a 24-expert mixture, with standard Transformers loading and published GGUF files. Its model card reports gains over its previous version on several zero-shot benchmarks; it is small enough to invite CPU or modest-GPU experimentation, though it is a text-completion model rather than a chat assistant.

ESP32 Remote Access Done Light

Pinscope's team replaced a full TLS connection with a Noise protocol handshake for an ESP32 that only needs to reach one known server. Their write-up reports a reduction in connection memory from 23.6 KB to 9.4 KB, with a 0.3-second handshake and replay protection—useful engineering for constrained devices, but a specialized design rather than a drop-in TLS replacement.

The bring-your-own-Pico carrier accepts Pico, Pico W, Pico 2, and Pico 2W modules, with a small display, buttons, microSD, EEPROM, and buzzer. Expansion options include Wi-Fi, LoRa, Ethernet, and NeoPixels, while the board is designed to be easier to hand-solder than earlier versions.

NASA’s NG-25 Mission to Launch Pioneering Space Crops Research

NASA says Northrop Grumman's NG-25 cargo mission will carry a prototype space-crop growth chamber to the International Space Station to test water delivery and volume optimization in microgravity. The mission also includes a miniature fluid-handling investigation for maintaining lab-grown brain tissue, linking practical food production with human-health research for future exploration.
