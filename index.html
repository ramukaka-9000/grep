REFORGE: LLM-assisted RTL repair with structured verification evidence

REFORGE repairs hardware-description-language bugs while retaining separate evidence for simulation, formal equivalence, security, and implementation cost. Across 288 runs, the authors report that requiring all applicable checks yields far fewer verified repairs than a testbench-only pass, a useful warning for anyone applying code-generating models to safety- or hardware-critical work.

How much effort does an AI code reviewer need?

This preregistered study tests three Claude 5.5 models on 90 agent-written Django patches, including plausible changes that pass ordinary tests but fail stronger checks. More review effort reduced acceptance of wrong patches, yet even the best settings accepted at least 45% of the incorrect ones, so the authors conclude AI review is not a dependable gate by itself.

Whisper small distilled for Serbian and Croatian speech

This Whisper-small derivative is distilled for Serbian and Croatian, with training on read and parliamentary speech. Its model card says the quantized Q8 build is about 264 MB and reports results close to Whisper large-v3-turbo on Serbian parliamentary speech; it also cautions that automatic language detection is unreliable, so users should set the language explicitly.

A 1.7-million-parameter Luganda text model trained on a MacBook

This tiny Luganda text-completion model has 1,695,232 parameters and was trained on 58,346 lines in about 75 seconds on an M1 Pro. Its author reports a held-out score of 1.5055 bits per character and is candid that the model only continues text, often wanders semantically, and is not an instruction-following assistant.

USB microphone recording and live metering on an ESP32-P4

A new CircuitPython USB-host audio module lets a Metro ESP32-P4 record from a USB microphone, draw a live waveform and level meter, and save WAV files to an SD card. The guide pairs the board with a Raspberry Pi Touch Display 2, where tapping the screen starts and stops recording.

Daisy-chained ESP32 stepper nodes use CAN, Wi-Fi, serial, or USB

Each compact node mounts on a NEMA 17 motor and combines an ESP32-S3 with a TMC2209 driver; commands can enter over USB, Wi-Fi, serial, or CAN and pass along the chain. The modular approach scales motor control without requiring every actuator to run back to a separate multi-channel controller.

An ESP32-S3 camera upgrade built through 42 firmware revisions

A Prusa Core One camera upgrade combines an autofocus ESP32-S3 camera with SD-card timelapse recording, then tackles exposure, digital zoom, and recovery from power loss. The author iterated through 42 firmware revisions and published the resulting firmware, a useful example of the work needed to make a maker-grade camera dependable in daily use.

NASA astronaut photographs the Eye of the Sahara

An International Space Station photograph made on Nov. 14 and published by NASA on Nov. 25 shows Mauritania’s Richat Structure, the 45-kilometer-wide formation nicknamed the Eye of the Sahara. NASA describes it as an eroded geological dome roughly 100 million years old, with older rocks exposed toward the center of its concentric rings.

NASA tests strawberries, cucumbers, and lemon basil for future space menus

NASA reports recent tests of strawberries, cucumbers, and lemon basil as possible fresh foods for future Moon and Mars crews, balancing nutrition and variety with the demands of growing crops in spacecraft habitats. The strawberry comparison found the wild variety produced more buds, flowers, and fruit, but its runners took too much room; cucumbers yielded edible fruit in spaceflight-condition trials, while lemon basil has passed preliminary seed-sanitization testing.
