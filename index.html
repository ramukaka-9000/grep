The Lean and Mathlib communities certified Levent Alpöge’s proof after checking the statement, compilation, and exported proof with independent checkers. Einstein’s relativity reshapes the textbook picture of a triple bond

Brown chemists report spectroscopic evidence that relativistic effects in a carbon–bismuth ion mix the sigma and pi components of a classical triple bond. The result confirms a prediction dating to the 1970s and shows why familiar bonding diagrams fail for very heavy elements.

Potatoes grown in simulated lunar soil match Earth-grown performance

NASA-supported researchers grew potatoes in lunar-regolith simulant amended with vermicompost. With sufficient organic amendment, plants performed comparably to Earth-soil controls and retained nutritional quality; this is a controlled simulation, not actual lunar soil.

A 194-year-old tortoise’s genome offers clues to extreme longevity

Researchers analyzed the genome of Jonathan, an Aldabra giant tortoise estimated to be 194, and identified variants linked to DNA repair, inflammation, insulin regulation, and cancer suppression. These associations suggest avenues for studying longevity, but do not establish a single cause of its lifespan.

Data centers’ long-distance damage to air quality

A model estimates that U.S. data-center electricity demand contributed to about 650 premature deaths in 2023, including impacts from fine particles and smog. The estimate captures health impacts far from facilities and is not a direct count of observed deaths.
