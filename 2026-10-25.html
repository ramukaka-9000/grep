EpiSEM-Bench: Evaluating Safety and Evidence Recovery in LLM Agents under Missing Evidence

The benchmark targets a subtle agent failure: a tool response can be entirely truthful yet omit a safety-critical fact, leading an agent to take an action it should not. EpiSEM-Bench packages 300 matched safe/unsafe task pairs across six domains to measure unsafe execution, boundary recognition, and evidence recovery. It gives agent builders a concrete way to test omission-sensitive behavior rather than relying only on overtly malicious prompts.

Can an Agent Debug a Web Corpus? Agentic Auditing of Slovak FineWeb2

Rather than ask an LLM to classify documents, the authors use a tool-using agent as a budgeted, read-only corpus debugger: it explores Slovak FineWeb2 and turns suspected problems into executable tests for a native speaker to validate. Three runs costing $2.21–$2.84 each surfaced seven findings across four phenomena in a 26.5-million-document subset; the largest was a machine-generated content farm comprising 5.32% of the corpus. The workflow is an unusually concrete example of agents helping audit training data while keeping validation in human hands.

Neural chip decodes interactions between brain regions

EPFL’s LogSync implantable system extracts oscillation strength and synchrony biomarkers across brain regions, then uses machine learning to classify cognitive or emotional states and trigger stimulation. The team reports that its co-designed chip performs this network-level processing at under 200 microwatts, using logarithmic computation to simplify the workload. The prototype is aimed at future closed-loop psychiatric therapies, not a clinical treatment already in use.

Sound Reactive Paper Lanterns with LedFx

The build combines paper lanterns, a Feather ESP32 running WLED, and the open-source LedFx application to animate lights from computer audio over Wi-Fi. Adafruit links to a full build guide, making this a reproducible way to add sound response without writing a custom audio-analysis pipeline. The useful detail is the division of labor: WLED handles the LEDs while LedFx supplies the effects.

Arduino UNO Q touch-shield library uses the ICSP header

A community library adds support for Adafruit’s 2.8-inch capacitive-touch TFT Shield v2 on the Arduino UNO Q. The practical hardware wrinkle is that the shield routes SPI through the six-pin ICSP header rather than the familiar pins 11–13; the target board uses an STM32U585 core. A narrowly scoped compatibility fix that could save other UNO Q owners from rediscovering the pin-mapping issue.

NASA’s asteroid Bennu samples reveal a 4.5-billion-year-old secret

Isotope measurements of iron, titanium, and chromium in returned Bennu material show a close chemical kinship with asteroid Ryugu and rare CI meteorites. The ETH Zürich team argues that Bennu’s parent material mixed dust from the inner and outer Solar System near the water-ice line, with early Jupiter’s growth helping shape that redistribution. It is a proposed formation scenario grounded in returned samples, not a direct observation of Jupiter moving the material.

NASA’s Artemis III Mission to Test Future Moon Lander Operations

NASA now describes Artemis III, planned for 2027, as a 14-day low-Earth-orbit test of rendezvous, docking, habitation, and operations with test versions of landers from Blue Origin and SpaceX. The agency says these demonstrations are meant to lower risk ahead of crewed lunar landings targeted for 2028. The changed mission architecture is a significant engineering and programmatic step, distinct from a claim that astronauts will land on the Moon during Artemis III.
