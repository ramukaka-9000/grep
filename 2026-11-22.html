Verify the Verifier: Specification-Grounded LLM Cross-Checking for Bugs That Escape UVM Scoreboards

The paper asks whether a passing hardware regression satisfies the specification, rather than merely matching what its scoreboard checks. Across 564 inserted bugs in four OpenTitan IPs, its authors identify 68 candidate scoreboard escapes and confirm 41 with specification-grounded checks, a concrete case for treating LLM analysis as a complement to formal assertions.

This systematization proposes an autonomy scale for comparing claims about LLMs finding, validating, and patching real software vulnerabilities. rust-anything: Rewrite your app in Rust, one piece at a time, checked against the original's recorded behavior

This MIT-licensed toolkit records an existing program's behavior and checks a Rust port against those recordings as each piece is rewritten. Its README reports a Prettier port with byte-identical output on 15,861 of 17,224 recorded runs, illustrating a practical compatibility-first migration workflow rather than blind translation.

This Apache-2.0 0.6B embedding model retrieves likely tools from large function or MCP catalogs, reducing the definitions an agent needs to load into context. A giant star swallowed another star. James Webb reveals the aftermath

Astronomers identified a rare merger in Andromeda in which a smaller star spiraled into a supergiant and left a single, possibly rejuvenated star. NASA's James Webb Space Telescope observed the aftermath 47 days after the eruption, giving researchers an unusually prompt view of a stellar merger's evolving debris.
